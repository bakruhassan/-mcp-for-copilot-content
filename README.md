Change 3: Keep the six capabilities, but reveal them only at the end
The feedback was that the six capabilities were not sufficiently obvious while the story was being presented. The recommendation was to number them and summarise them clearly at the end.

You can still retain:

Chief of Staff
Invisible Attendee
Executive Coach
Decision Partner
Incident Investigator
Transformation Advisor
But do not position them as six separate presentation sections.

Instead, finish with:

Throughout Maya’s day, AI played six different roles.
Maya did not use six different tools.
Maya strengthened three leadership capabilities: focus, judgement and transformation.

Change 4: Use video as reinforcement, not as the entire session
The video can be valuable after the event as a reusable reference, but the meeting feedback leaned towards showing leaders how to do the activities and giving them opportunities to try.

Better use of the video
Use a short video clip within each module
Keep each clip focused on the exact interaction
Pause immediately after it
Let leaders try one question themselves
Keep the full continuous Maya video as a post-session resource
This prevents the 45-minute session from becoming passive viewing.
